The plan

Two 12-week blocks: Block 1 runs Monday, Nov 30, 2026 to Sunday, Feb 21, 2027, and Block 2 runs Feb 22 to May 16, 2027. Five lifting days a week: Lower A (squat), Upper A (bench), Lower B (deadlift), Upper B and Upper C. Weekends are rest days.

Like Dad's plan, the workouts rotate one day each week, so each one lands on a different weekday. Deadlifts alternate trap bar and conventional, and Walking Lunges and Goblet Squats alternate, week to week.

Each block: Foundation (Weeks 1 to 4), Strength (5 to 8), Power (9 to 11) and a lighter Week 12. Block 2 starts about 15% lighter for more reps, then builds again. Speed and agility drills start with spring and summer camp.

Each workout takes about 45 to 60 minutes.

How target weights work

Before Week 1, tap Start and enter a starting weight for each lift. After that, the big red number is the target. Tap it to type your own; tap Reset to go back to automatic.

Hit the top of the rep range on every set with good form and the next target goes up 5 lb; otherwise it stays the same. Log the reps from his weakest set. Lighter weeks drop to about 80%. The first week of a new phase starts about 10% heavier for fewer reps.

Dumbbell weights are per dumbbell. Core and carry exercises (Pallof press, wood chopper, cable crunch, back extension, farmer carry) have no automatic target: log the weight and go a little heavier when it feels easy.

Safety

Learn the barbell lifts (squat, bench, RDL) from a qualified coach or YMCA trainer before going heavy, and always use a spotter or safety bars on squat and bench. Form beats weight every time.

Stop a set if form breaks down, and stop the session for sharp or joint pain. Check the YMCA's rules for 16-year-olds in the weight room.

He's still growing, so this is not a diet. He should eat enough to train hard and recover: regular meals with protein, fruits and vegetables. The daily habits cover the basics. Talk with his doctor about weight goals.

Body weight and habits

Weigh in once a month on the Body tab (Nov 30, Jan 4, Feb 1, Mar 1, Apr 5, May 3). There are no targets. Each day also has 5 habits to check off: breakfast, protein at every meal, water, 8+ hours of sleep and no sugary drinks.

Bike rides at home

Three days a week (normally Mon, Wed and Fri), a TrainerRoad ride is added at the end of the workout, done at home after lifting. Rides never land on a leg day; when Mon, Wed or Fri is a leg day, the ride moves to the next upper-body day. Days with a ride show "+Bike" on the tab.

Pick one: Taku -1 (30 min), Lazy Mountain -2 (45 min), Recess -4 (45 min), Lazy Mountain (60 min) or Pettit (60 min). Ride easy enough to talk in full sentences. Workout names in TrainerRoad's library can change; pick a similar Endurance ride if one isn't there.

Back up your log

The log is saved only on this phone. Save a backup every week or two, and before deleting the app or clearing Safari data. Either file type restores everything; the .csv also opens in Google Sheets or Excel. Saving opens your device's save or share options: on iPhone choose Save to Files (or Google Drive, Dropbox and similar), on Android pick Drive or Files.

To restore, tap Restore from backup and browse to the .json or .csv file you saved (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Downloads). This replaces everything currently in the app.
